About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple web app for capturing short notes and ideas the moment you think of them. It helps you keep your reminders in one place so nothing gets forgotten.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the QuickNotes home page.
  2. Type your note into the text box.
  3. Click the "Add Note" button or press Enter.
  4. See your new note appear in the "Your notes" list.

Features

  • Add a note in seconds
  • See all your notes in one list
  • Works on phones and computers
  • Simple, clean and easy to use

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
